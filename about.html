GOO DAARI is being built to make local discovery simpler — one place to find useful businesses, services, rentals, professionals and community needs around you.

What is GOO DAARI?

In many towns, people know someone who can help — an electrician, AC technician, mechanic, photographer, JCB operator, caterer, shop owner or other local provider. The challenge is finding the right person when you need them.

GOO DAARI brings those local options into one searchable platform. Our goal is to make it easier for customers to discover local providers and easier for genuine local businesses to get discovered.

Find Local. Get It Done.

Our approach

We are starting locally and growing location by location instead of trying to cover everything at once.

Initial locations include Peddapuram and Samalkota. The platform is designed to expand into Pithapuram, Kakinada, Rajamahendravaram and additional nearby towns as listings are added.

A location can exist in the platform before its directory is fully populated. More local businesses can be added over time.
