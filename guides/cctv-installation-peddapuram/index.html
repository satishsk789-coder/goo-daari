Key questions about cameras, storage, installation, remote viewing and after-sales support.

Before you contact a provider

Start by describing the exact job, location and preferred timing. Ask whether the provider serves your area, whether a visit or inspection fee applies, what is included in the quoted price and what warranty or follow-up support is offered.

Compare practical details

  • Confirm service area and availability.
  • Ask for an itemised estimate where relevant.
  • Clarify materials, equipment or parts included.
  • Confirm expected completion time.
  • Keep the provider's contact details and quotation for reference.
