Local services, businesses and rentals — all in one place

GOO DAARI helps people discover local businesses, services, rentals and professionals across the Godavari region. The platform is designed to expand location by location, starting with available listings in Peddapuram and Samalkota and growing into more towns over time.

What is GOO DAARI?

GOO DAARI is a local discovery platform that helps people find businesses, services, rentals and professionals in the locations available on the platform.

Which locations are available?

Use the location selector to browse available towns. More East Godavari locations can be added as local listings grow.

Can I list my business?

Yes. Use the List Your Business page to submit your business details.

How do I contact a business?

Where contact information is available, you can call, use WhatsApp or open directions from a listing.
