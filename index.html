Local services, businesses and rentals — all in one place

GOO DAARI is a local discovery platform for finding businesses, home services, rentals, events, electronics and other useful services across the Godavari region. We are starting with Peddapuram and Samalkota, with more locations such as Pithapuram, Kakinada and Rajamahendravaram being added as local listings grow. Search for an electrician, AC repair technician, JCB rental, photographer, CCTV service, computer repair, local shop and more.

Frequently asked questions

What is GOO DAARI?

GOO DAARI helps people discover local businesses and service providers in and around Peddapuram.

Which areas does GOO DAARI cover?

Choose a location from the header. We are starting with Peddapuram and Samalkota, with Pithapuram, Kakinada and Rajamahendravaram planned as the directory expands.

Can I list my business?

Yes. Local businesses can submit their details through the List Your Business page.

Can I contact a business directly?

Where a public phone number is available, you can call or use WhatsApp from the listing.
