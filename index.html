GOO DAARI is built to make local discovery simpler. Search for home services, rentals, events, electronics, transport, shops and professionals by location. Peddapuram and Samalkota are currently part of the live network, while more towns across East Godavari are being prepared for expansion.

Frequently asked questions

What is GOO DAARI?

GOO DAARI is a local discovery platform for finding businesses, services, rentals and professionals by location.

Which locations are available?

Use the location selector to choose a town. The network will expand location by location as local listings are added and verified.

Can I list my business?

Yes. Local business owners can submit their details through the List Your Business page.

How do I contact a listing?

Where a public phone number is available, you can call or use WhatsApp. Directions can also open in Google Maps.
