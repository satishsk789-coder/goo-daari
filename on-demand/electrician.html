COMMON PROBLEMS, SOLVED

Tell us the problem. We bring the fix.

The most common electrical issues households in Peddapuram call us for, and how a GOO DAARI electrician handles each one.

🌀

Fan not working or too slow

Solution: Electrician checks the capacitor, regulator and winding on-site — most fan issues are fixed the same visit, no need to replace the whole fan.

💡

Lights flickering or not turning on

Solution: Checked for loose wiring, faulty holders or a failing switch, and replaced or re-fixed on the spot with genuine parts you approve first.

🔌

Switch board or socket not working

Solution: Board is opened, wiring tested for continuity, and the faulty switch, socket or board is replaced safely without disturbing the rest of the circuit.

⚠️

Frequent power trips / MCB drops

Solution: Electrician inspects load on each circuit, checks for earthing faults and short circuits before resetting — so the trip doesn't just come back tomorrow.

🏠

New wiring point for AC, geyser or appliance

Solution: A dedicated point is wired with the correct rated cable and MCB for that appliance, so it isn't sharing load with your regular room circuit.

🛠️

Old house rewiring or renovation work

Solution: Electrician visits, assesses the existing wiring condition, and gives you a clear plan and confirmed cost before any wiring is opened up.
