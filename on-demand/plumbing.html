COMMON PROBLEMS, SOLVED

Tell us the problem. We bring the fix.

The most common plumbing issues households across East Godavari call us for, and how a GOO DAARI professional handles each one.

💧

Tap or pipe leaking

Solution: Plumber checks the washer, joint and pipe condition on-site, replaces worn parts and pressure-tests before leaving.

🚽

Blocked drain or toilet

Solution: Cleared with proper rodding and plunging tools, not just a chemical dump — checked for a recurring blockage cause.

🚿

Low or no water pressure

Solution: Checked at the tap, the line and the motor or tank to find where the pressure is actually being lost.

🛁

New bathroom fitting installation

Solution: Wash basin, geyser point, shower or tap fitted and leak-tested before the job is called done.

🛠️

Water tank or motor issues

Solution: Motor, float valve and tank connections inspected to fix overflow, no-fill or noisy motor problems.

🏚️

Old pipeline leaks or renovation

Solution: Plumber assesses old GI or PVC lines and gives a clear replacement plan and cost before opening any wall.
