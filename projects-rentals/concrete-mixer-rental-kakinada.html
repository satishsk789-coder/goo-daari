What is ready-mix concrete and how is it delivered?

Ready-mix concrete is produced at a batching plant to a specified mix design and delivered to the project in a rotating-drum transit mixer truck. The supplier, project engineer and site team should coordinate the approved mix, quantity, delivery sequence, slump or workability requirements, sampling and placement method before the pour begins.

RMC supply for residential, commercial and civil projects

Typical enquiries include foundations, columns, beams, slabs, driveways, industrial floors, roads, drains and other concrete works. The required grade and mix design must come from the project’s structural engineer or approved specification. Do not choose a grade based only on a general online recommendation.

How to estimate and communicate your requirement

Share the concrete grade or approved mix designation, approximate volume in cubic metres, pour location, delivery date and time window, expected rate of placement and whether multiple trips are required. Mention pump requirements, pipeline distance, site turning space, overhead clearance and approach-road condition.

What affects RMC pricing and delivery?

The quotation may depend on grade, quantity, plant distance, transport, minimum order, pump hire, waiting time, delivery slot, additives or special mix requirements and cancellation terms. Ask for an itemised written quote and confirm how short loads, delays and unused concrete are handled.

Quality, testing and site coordination

Confirm supplier credentials, approved mix documentation, batch/delivery tickets, sampling and testing arrangements as specified by the project engineer. Ensure formwork, reinforcement, access, labour, pump placement and curing arrangements are ready before dispatch. Concrete placement and testing should be supervised by competent project personnel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this for a portable concrete mixer machine?

No. This page focuses on ready-mix concrete supply delivered by transit mixer trucks. Portable on-site drum mixers are a separate equipment category.

What unit should I use for quantity?

Share the estimated requirement in cubic metres (m³), as calculated or confirmed by your project team.

Can I request a concrete pump with RMC?

You can mention a pump requirement in the enquiry. Pump availability, line length, mobilisation and charges must be confirmed separately by the supplier.

Who decides the concrete grade?

The project’s qualified structural engineer or approved specification should determine the required grade and mix design.
