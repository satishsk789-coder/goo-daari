How to get a useful quotation

To help providers respond accurately, include the service you need, project or delivery address, expected quantity, equipment capacity, start date and expected duration. For construction work, share drawings or a written scope only after discussing how the documents will be used. For materials, mention the brand or specification, quantity, delivery access and unloading requirement.

Compare more than the headline price

  • Confirm whether transport, operator, fuel, loading, unloading and waiting time are included.
  • For material supply, compare brand, grade, quantity, taxes, delivery and return conditions.
  • For project contracts, document scope, milestones, materials responsibility, workmanship expectations and payment stages.
  • For machinery and rentals, check operating condition, safety arrangements, insurance and breakdown support.
