Scaffolding and formwork rental options

Depending on the project, requirements may include frame scaffolding, cuplock or other modular systems, working platforms, guardrails, base plates, adjustable steel props, centring plates and formwork accessories. Describe whether the need is for façade access, internal work, slab support or concrete formwork; these are different applications and may require different equipment.

Plan quantities and rental duration

Provide approximate working height, length and width, number of working levels, slab area, expected rental start and return dates, and whether erection or dismantling support is required. Ask the supplier to prepare an itemised list of frames, braces, platforms, props and accessories rather than quoting only a broad package.

Safety checks before use

Scaffolding and temporary works must be selected, erected, inspected and used by competent personnel under applicable safety requirements. Confirm load rating, condition, compatible components, bracing, ties, access, guardrails, safe foundations and inspection arrangements. Never improvise with mismatched or damaged components.

Rental charges and logistics

Clarify transport, loading/unloading, erection/dismantling, minimum rental duration, extension rates, missing or damaged component charges, deposit and return conditions. Photograph the supplied inventory at dispatch and return, and record quantities in writing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rent only adjustable props or centring plates?

Ask for the exact item and quantity. Some suppliers may rent individual components while others may offer complete packages.

Does the rental include erection?

Not necessarily. Confirm whether erection, dismantling, inspection and trained labour are included or separately priced.

How is damage or missing equipment charged?

Agree on an inventory, condition record, deposit and written replacement/repair rates before dispatch.
