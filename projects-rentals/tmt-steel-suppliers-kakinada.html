TMT bars for RCC construction

TMT reinforcement bars are used in reinforced concrete structures such as footings, columns, beams, slabs and retaining elements. The correct grade, diameter, spacing and quantity must follow the structural drawings and engineer-approved bar bending schedule. Never select reinforcement solely by price or substitute a specified grade without professional approval.

Details suppliers need to quote accurately

Share required diameters (for example, as specified in your drawings), grade, preferred make, approximate weight or number of bundles, cut-length requirements, delivery address and unloading arrangements. If you have a bar bending schedule, use it to prepare the enquiry and avoid estimating structural quantities from a generic online guide.

Compare steel quotations carefully

Ask suppliers to state brand, grade, applicable standard, diameter, weight basis, taxes, transport, unloading and validity period. Confirm whether the quote is per kilogram, tonne or bundle and how actual delivered weight is documented. Request a tax invoice and relevant product test or quality documents where applicable.

Receiving and storing reinforcement steel

Check the delivery against the order, invoice and bundle markings. Store bars off the ground and organise them by diameter and grade. Follow the engineer’s instructions for rust assessment, cutting, bending, lapping and placement.
