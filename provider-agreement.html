GOO DAARI · LEGAL DOCUMENT

Service Provider / Fulfilment Partner Agreement

Effective date: 18 October 2026

Important: This is the operational agreement draft used for GOO DAARI onboarding. Before relying on it as the final executed legal document, GOO DAARI should have the exact legal entity name/address, commercial schedule, tax treatment, stamp/e-stamp requirements and dispute-resolution venue reviewed by an Indian lawyer/CA.

1. Parties

This Agreement is between GOO DAARI, being the legal entity that operates the GOO DAARI platform (“GOO DAARI”), and the service provider identified in the Provider Registration Form (“Provider”).

2. Appointment and relationship

GOO DAARI may appoint the Provider as an independent fulfilment partner to perform services assigned through GOO DAARI for customers. The Provider is not an employee, officer, franchisee, partner, joint-venture participant or general legal agent of GOO DAARI and may not bind GOO DAARI except where expressly authorised in writing.

3. Scope of services

The Provider shall perform only the services assigned through a GOO DAARI Job Order, customer request, approved quotation or other written/electronic instruction. The Provider shall follow agreed specifications, pricing, service standards and safety requirements.

4. Provider eligibility and verification

The Provider shall provide accurate identity, address, contact, tax, bank/UPI, licence and qualification information as applicable. GOO DAARI may verify submitted information and may request reasonable additional information where necessary for safety, compliance, service allocation or dispute resolution.

5. Service standards and customer conduct

  • Arrive within the agreed time or promptly notify GOO DAARI of delay.
  • Use appropriate tools, equipment and PPE and maintain professional conduct.
  • Protect customer property and leave the work area reasonably clean.
  • No intoxication, harassment, threats, discrimination, theft, fraud or abusive conduct.
  • Do not perform additional chargeable work without the required customer/GOO DAARI approval.
  • Do not make misleading statements about GOO DAARI or claim to be its owner, director or employee.

6. Pricing and payment model

Commercial terms may be set in the applicable Job Order or commercial schedule. The recommended standard launch model is a GOO DAARI service fee/commission of 20% and Provider share of 80% for standard labour-oriented jobs, unless a different rate is agreed for a category, material-heavy job or special assignment. Materials are reimbursed/paid according to the approved Job Order.

The preferred payment flow is customer → GOO DAARI → Provider. GOO DAARI may pay the Provider after job completion and customer confirmation, normally within the agreed payout period, subject to genuine disputes, refunds, reversals, lawful deductions and applicable tax withholding. If the Provider is authorised to collect customer payment, the Provider must record the amount and remit GOO DAARI’s share within the agreed period and may not impose undisclosed charges.

7. Taxes and statutory compliance

Each party is responsible for its own applicable taxes, registrations, licences and statutory obligations. GOO DAARI may withhold or report amounts where required by applicable law. The parties shall provide reasonable information needed for lawful tax and reporting compliance.

8. Materials, tools and subcontracting

Unless a Job Order says otherwise, the Provider supplies ordinary tools, transport and PPE. Materials must be approved where required and supported by reasonable bills/records. The Provider may not subcontract, substitute personnel or delegate a job without GOO DAARI’s approval where such approval is required. Approved assistants remain the Provider’s responsibility.

9. Completion, workmanship and callback

The Provider shall confirm completion through the required GOO DAARI process. Unless a Job Order states otherwise, the default workmanship callback period is 30 days for defects caused by the Provider’s workmanship. This does not cover misuse, third-party interference, pre-existing defects, normal wear or manufacturer warranty issues. If the Provider fails a valid callback, GOO DAARI may arrange reasonable corrective work and seek recovery of the reasonable incremental cost, subject to applicable law and the Agreement.

10. Safety and special-risk work

For electrical, gas, height, welding, structural, chemical, heavy-equipment, lifting, driving or other higher-risk work, the Provider must use appropriately qualified personnel, required licences and suitable safety measures. The Provider must stop and escalate work that appears unsafe or outside its competence.

11. Customer data and privacy

Customer information supplied for a GOO DAARI job may be used only for authorised fulfilment and related operational purposes. The Provider must keep it secure, must not sell or disclose it, must not add customers to personal marketing lists without a lawful basis/permission, and must report suspected data loss or unauthorised access to GOO DAARI promptly.

12. Non-circumvention and customer protection

The Provider shall not deliberately bypass GOO DAARI by asking a customer to cancel a GOO DAARI job and deal privately for the same or substantially similar service, or misuse GOO DAARI customer information for unauthorised solicitation. Any post-assignment restriction must be interpreted and enforced only to the extent permitted by applicable law.

13. Damage, loss, injury and indemnity

The Provider is responsible, subject to applicable law, for loss or damage caused by its negligence, misconduct, defective workmanship, unauthorised acts, fraud, theft, safety violations or the acts of its approved assistants. The Provider shall indemnify GOO DAARI against third-party claims, losses, reasonable legal costs, penalties or damages arising from such breach or conduct to the extent legally recoverable.

14. Insurance

Where the service or law reasonably requires insurance, the Provider shall maintain appropriate insurance and provide evidence when requested.

15. Branding and representation

The Provider may use the GOO DAARI name, logo, uniform or ID only for authorised GOO DAARI work and according to GOO DAARI instructions. The Provider shall not represent itself as the owner, director, employee or authorised spokesperson of GOO DAARI.

16. Records, complaints and investigations

The Provider shall maintain reasonable records of jobs, quotations, materials and payments and cooperate with reasonable customer complaints, quality checks, fraud reviews and safety investigations. GOO DAARI may temporarily suspend assignments while investigating serious complaints or safety/fraud concerns.

17. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Non-public GOO DAARI information, customer information, commercial terms, internal processes and credentials must be kept confidential. GOO DAARI retains its rights in its brand, website, software, customer lists and platform materials. Work photographs or service information supplied by the Provider may be used by GOO DAARI for legitimate operational or marketing purposes only where appropriate permissions and applicable law permit.

18. Suspension and termination

Either party may terminate in accordance with the applicable commercial schedule or written notice. GOO DAARI may immediately suspend or terminate access for serious safety risk, fraud, theft, violence/harassment, unlawful conduct, unauthorised data use, deliberate circumvention, repeated no-shows, loss of required licence, material breach or serious reputational risk. Termination does not remove accrued payment obligations or continuing confidentiality, data, indemnity or dispute provisions.

19. Independent contractor

The Provider is responsible for its own business expenses, personnel, taxes, registrations, insurance and statutory obligations. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship or entitlement to employee benefits.

20. Electronic acceptance

The Provider agrees that electronic records, online acceptance, OTP/e-signature where used, emails, WhatsApp messages and electronic Job Orders may evidence instructions and acceptance to the extent permitted by applicable law.

21. Dispute resolution and governing law

This Agreement is governed by applicable Indian law. The parties should first attempt good-faith resolution of a dispute. If unresolved, disputes may be referred to arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, by a sole arbitrator appointed in accordance with that law. The final agreement should specify the agreed seat/venue and competent courts after legal review.

22. General

If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to the extent permitted by law. Amendments should be recorded in writing or through an electronic Job Order/commercial schedule. The Provider shall keep contact and payment information current.

Provider electronic acceptance

By checking the agreement acceptance box on the GOO DAARI Provider Registration Form and submitting the form, the Provider confirms that they have read this Agreement and agree to its applicable terms, subject to final legal review and the agreed commercial schedule.
