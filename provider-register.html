Key terms summary

Appointment

You may be appointed as an independent fulfilment partner to perform assigned services for GOO DAARI customers. You are not an employee, officer, franchisee or joint-venture partner of GOO DAARI.

Service responsibility

You must perform assigned work professionally, safely, lawfully and with reasonable care, using qualified personnel and appropriate tools/PPE.

Customer payments & payout

Unless a Job Order states otherwise, the preferred model is that the customer pays GOO DAARI and GOO DAARI pays the provider after completion and customer confirmation, subject to applicable deductions, disputes and refunds. Commercial rates are defined by the applicable Job Order / commercial schedule.

Customer protection

No undisclosed charges, deliberate bypassing of GOO DAARI, misuse of customer information, harassment, fraud, theft or unauthorized work is permitted.

Verification & data

Information and documents may be verified for onboarding, safety, service allocation, payment, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Provider data must be kept confidential and used only for authorized GOO DAARI work.

Damage, negligence & compliance

The provider remains responsible for negligent work, misconduct, unauthorized acts, property damage, injury caused by the provider or assistants, and applicable legal/tax/licensing obligations attributable to the provider.

Termination

GOO DAARI may suspend or terminate access for serious safety issues, fraud, theft, harassment, unauthorized data use, deliberate circumvention, repeated no-shows, material breach or illegal activity.

Legal terms

The complete agreement governs the relationship. It is subject to applicable Indian law and contains dispute-resolution, confidentiality, records and electronic-acceptance provisions.
